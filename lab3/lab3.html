Improving Readability with CSS

Why Typography Matters

Good typography makes text easier to scan and understand. Choosing a clean font and a comfortable size helps readers stay focused on the content instead of struggling with the layout.

Line spacing is just as important as font size. When lines are packed too tightly, the eye has trouble moving from one line to the next, especially in longer paragraphs.

Tips for Readable Pages

A few simple habits can noticeably improve any web page. The list below summarizes the most useful ones.

  • Use a sans-serif font for body text
  • Keep paragraph text at 16px or larger
  • Set a line height between 1.5 and 1.6
  • Choose colors with strong contrast
